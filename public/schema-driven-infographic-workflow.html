The Handoff

The division of labor that makes everything possible. Reid excels at thinking and scoping. Claude excels at building. Neither is great at the other's job.

Method 1

tmux Orchestration

Reid controls Claude Code programmatically. Send prompts, monitor progress, extract results. Full automation when the task is well-scoped.

Method 2

Prompt Handoff

Reid writes a detailed prompt, Dave copy/pastes to Claude Code. Simple, effective. Human in the loop when judgment matters.

Why this works: Flat-rate Max subscription means Claude can work all day without cost anxiety. Reid provides the context and judgment. Claude provides the sustained generation. The handoff IS the architecture.

Design Rationale — Version A: The Pipeline

Visual Metaphor

Assembly line. Each phase is a station on the factory floor. Work moves left-to-right, top-to-bottom through a predictable sequence. The Curate gate is literally painted red — a physical barrier that stops the line.

Emphasis

Process and predictability. This version says: "We have a system, and the system works." The linear flow makes the sequence obvious and the gates unmissable.

Tradeoffs

Linear layouts can feel rigid. The creative energy of 3-lens exploration is somewhat constrained by the box format. But for someone trying to understand "what happens in what order," nothing beats a pipeline.

Best For

Technical audiences who want to understand the process. Engineers, CTOs, anyone who thinks in systems and sequences.

Design Rationale — Version B: The Conversation

Visual Metaphor

The GitHub Issue as campfire. Everything gathers around it. Phases flow toward and away from the central artifact. The issue starts work, tracks state, captures findings, and closes the loop.

Emphasis

Collaboration and the issue as organizing principle. The labels visually connect phases to issue state. The preparation/execution split creates a natural rhythm above and below the hub.

Tradeoffs

The sequence is less obvious than Version A. You lose some linearity to gain centrality. But for teams that live in GitHub Issues, this immediately clicks.

Best For

GitHub-native teams. Product managers. Anyone who thinks in issues and labels rather than pipelines and flowcharts.

Design Rationale — Version C: The Game Board

Visual Metaphor

Board game path. Phases are spaces you move through. The serpentine layout (row 1 left-to-right, row 2 right-to-left) creates a natural game-board S-curve. Explore is the multi-lane stretch. Curate is the checkpoint. Compound is the level-up.

Emphasis

Progression and strategy. Each space is a step forward. The U-turn creates visual continuity between the two rows. The three-lens tags on Explore show the branching explicitly.

Tradeoffs

The reverse-order second row requires a moment to parse. But board game conventions are deeply familiar — most people intuitively read serpentine layouts correctly. The playful energy might not suit every audience.

Best For

Visual thinkers. People who respond to game mechanics and progression. Teams who want to feel the "level up" moment of compounding knowledge.

Constitutional Principles

Seven rules that govern all schema and implementation decisions.

1

Spec-First, Always

No code changes without corresponding spec updates. Specs are the source of truth.

2

Idempotent from Schema

Application is a deterministic function of specs. Undocumented behavior is a bug.

3

Bidirectional Traceability

story.flowIds ↔ flow.implementsStories. Validated in CI.

4

Config-Driven Behavior

Business rules live in JSON, not code. Owners update rules directly.

5

Integration Contract

Reference external entities, don't reinvent them. Define mappings on top.

6

Compounding

Learnings feed back into specs. Every solved problem makes the next easier.

7

Fail-Closed Security

Unmatched items flagged for review, never silently classified. Deny by default.

Two Schema Layers

Meta-schemas define structure. Domain schemas define the business. Everything else is governance, instances, or mechanisms.

Layer 1: Meta-Schemas Define Structure

Schemas that define the shape of all other specs. These are the grammar of the system.

Not Schema Layers

Important, but distinct from the schema taxonomy.

Governance

Constitutional principles are rules that govern, not schemas. They constrain how schemas evolve.

Rules, not structure

Instances

User stories and flows are data conforming to meta-schemas. Content, not container.

Data, not schemas

Validation

A cross-cutting mechanism — a quality gate. It enforces conformance; it isn't a schema.

Mechanism, not layer

Custom x- annotations that make schemas actionable.
